About your Coach

Why choose me as your Personal Guide to UI/UX Mastery

Hey! I’m Mariam Parvez, here to help you dive into UI/UX design and dev with either 1:1 sessions or my online course, updated every Wednesday!

With six years in the field—from Deloitte to Insoft—I’ve packed this course with real-world skills and hands-on projects. We’ll cover everything from basics to pro techniques, keeping it practical and straightforward, no fluff.

If you’re ready for a truly personal, no-nonsense dive into design, let’s get started!
